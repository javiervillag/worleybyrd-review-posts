# ⭐ WorleyByrd Review Posts

**Turn every new 5-star review WorleyByrd Roofing & Restoration gets into a ready-to-post Instagram image and caption, in about 5 minutes a week.**

It's a Claude skill that's already set up for WorleyByrd: logo, colors, fonts, background photos and review links are built in.

---

## Setup (5 minutes, one time)

### Step 1: Download this file

👉 **[Download `worleybyrd-review-posts.zip`](https://github.com/javiervillag/worleybyrd-review-posts/raw/main/worleybyrd-review-posts.zip)**

Leave it as a .zip. Don't unzip it.

### Step 2: Add it to Claude

We recommend the **Claude desktop app with Cowork** ([free download](https://claude.com/download)). Cowork can open your review pages and grab the new reviews for you.

1. In Claude, click **Customize**, then **Skills**.
2. Click **+**, then **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**.
3. Pick **`worleybyrd-review-posts.zip`**.
4. Make sure its switch is **on**.
5. Go to **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, and turn on **"Code execution and file creation"** (it may be called "Create and edit files").

> 💡 **On the free Claude plan?** It still works. Paste the new reviews into the chat yourself instead of Claude opening the review pages. Cowork and the browser features need a paid plan (Pro or higher).

<details>
<summary><b>Using ChatGPT instead? Click here.</b></summary>

- **If your ChatGPT has "Skills"** (Business, Enterprise or Edu plans): open **Skills**, click **Create**, then **Upload from your computer**, and pick the zip.
- **If you don't see "Skills":** start a new chat, attach the zip with the paperclip, and type: **"Unzip this and follow the SKILL.md inside to make WorleyByrd review posts."** Then paste in your new reviews.

## Every week: just ask

> **Make review posts**

It checks Google, the WorleyByrd website testimonials and BBB, then gives you:
- 🖼️ **Posts for new 5-star reviews only.** Reviews already turned into posts are skipped.
- ✍️ **A caption** for each post
- 📋 **An approval sheet** that opens in Excel or Google Sheets, with Approve and Posted columns

**It never posts anything.** You choose what goes out and when.

---

## Good to know

- 📸 **New background photos:** send Claude new house or roof photos at any time ("use these as backgrounds") and they rotate in. Use photos that show **homes and roofs**, not downtown skylines. Drone and phone photos work best; avoid screenshots. Photo credits are in [PHOTO-CREDITS.md](worleybyrd-review-posts/business/photos/PHOTO-CREDITS.md).
- 📷 **Customer photos:** if a reviewer attached photos of their roof, that photo becomes the background for their post.
- ✏️ **Word for word:** reviews are never rewritten. The one exception is BBB, which hides staff names. Those become a plain phrase like "The owner", and every change is listed in the approval sheet.
- ⭐ **5-star only:** 4-star and lower reviews are never used.
- 🎨 **Change the look:** say *"use the review card look"*, *"no two-slide posts"*, or *"fix the crop on post 3"*.
